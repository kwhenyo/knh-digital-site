Our Services

What Service We Offer

One managed practice, two delivery lanes — run with the same discipline whether the deliverable is a website or a content calendar.

The umbrella

Project Management

PMP- and Scrum-led delivery for digital and technical projects — scope, schedule, stakeholders, and risk, on every engagement below, including AI strategy work.

Sub-service

Website Design & Management

Design and build on a managed timeline, then ongoing upkeep — content updates, hosting, security patching — so the site keeps working after launch.

Website Delivery
  • Setup (domain, hosting, site structure)
  • Design & build (pages, content, mobile-responsive)
  • Launch & handoff (testing, go-live, walkthrough)
Sub-service

Digital & Social Media Management

Planned content calendars, scheduled posting, and account management across the platforms your audience is actually on — run with the same cadence and reporting as any managed project.

Social Media Delivery
  • Setup (profile, highlights, pinned posts)
  • 7–14 day content pack (reels/TikTok + feed + stories)
  • Optional ongoing posting
