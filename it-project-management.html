IT Project Management and PMO Support

Structured project delivery for IT initiatives using Scrum, Waterfall, or hybrid methods. We build the schedule, track the risks, run the meetings, and report status so leadership always knows where the project stands.

Who it’s for: businesses and organizations running IT projects without a full-time project manager. We support clients in Virginia, the Washington, DC metro area, and remotely.

What we do

Project and program management

  • IT project and program management, including AI adoption and tool rollout projects
  • PMO support and governance support
  • Project planning, scheduling, and requirements tracking
  • Implementation coordination and transition support

Scheduling and tracking

  • Microsoft Project schedule development and updates
  • Milestone and dependency tracking
  • Critical-path monitoring
  • Release tracking and phase-gate planning

Reporting and governance

  • Weekly and monthly status reporting
  • Excel-based project reporting and dashboards
  • Risk, issue, assumption, and dependency (RAID) tracking
  • Executive and management reporting
  • Meeting coordination and action-item tracking

Agile and Scrum support

  • Sprint planning and backlog coordination
  • Scrum ceremonies: planning, daily stand-ups, reviews, and retrospectives
  • Release tracking across sprints
