Who we are

KNH Digital LLC is a Virginia technology services company based in Arlington, registered in 2022. We provide IT project management and PMO support, IT migration and upgrade coordination, process documentation, and website design and development.

Led by Kwami Henyo, PMP

KNH Digital is led by founder Kwami Henyo, a PMP- and Scrum-certified project manager with a background in IT infrastructure, migrations, and upgrades. He holds an M.S. in Cybersecurity and a B.S. in Information Systems Management.

How we work

  • One accountable project manager: not a handoff between subcontractors.
  • Visible progress: every engagement runs on a real scope, schedule, and status report.
  • Plain language: clear updates on what is done, what is next, and what is at risk.
